# Urdu review list - 304 (LinkUp 3.0.100+171)

For a native Pakistani Urdu speaker. Every app text is listed below, English next to its Urdu.
Mark wrong or unnatural words; the fix goes into the next build.

Rules used:
- Modern spoken Pakistani Urdu, short. English words where people use them: کال، میسج، پروفائل، سیٹنگز، اپ ڈیٹ، سرور، پاس ورڈ، کوڈ، وائی فائی، ایڈمن، یوزر نیم، بیک اپ، QR، ڈیوائس.
- Not translated: LinkUp, names, @usernames, "My Android Phone", phone settings menu names (they match the phone's own English menus), the PC web dashboard.
- People are spoken of with respect (ہیں / کرتے ہیں), so "he/she" is not needed.
- Times stay "5:40 PM"; digits stay 0-9; the layout stays left-to-right (only the words are Urdu).

- Texts 183-190 (e.g. "below."): sentence parts (bold words between); joined right-to-left with L.spans - check the whole sentence on screen
- Texts 880-897 (e.g. "1. App info > Battery: choose Unrestricted"): phone settings menu names kept in English (they match the phone's own English menus); only the verbs are Urdu
- "video dbg · {0} · rx:{1} · {2}" → "video dbg · {0} · rx:{1} · {2}": debug line kept in English
